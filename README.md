# Agentic Software Engineering Workshop Series

A free public online workshop series hosted by **Dev and Design HQ**

Students from the Ship and Found Bootcamp present live, hands-on technical
demonstrations to a public audience. Each presenter builds something
in front of the room and takes questions afterwards.

Agentic software engineering means building software by directing AI coding
agents and checking their work, instead of writing every line by hand.

Website: https://devdesignhq.com

This repository is the public record of the series. It holds the event records,
presenter records, attendance figures, links to recordings, and the certificate
register.

A 90 minute public online workshop. 240 people registered and 140 attended
live, a 58% attendance rate.

**Guest speaker:** Julian Duru, Lead Researcher, Flow Research, an AI research
company based in Africa. Julian spoke on AI, the direction of agentic coding,

## Verifying a presenter's claim

If you are checking a claim made by one of our presenters, for an application,
an admission, a role or a visa, everything you need is public.

1. **The recording.** Public on YouTube, with chapter markers naming each
   presenter. Link here : https://youtu.be/k9S5nDGPigo?si=oKgdPQ9RmZaUMzO7
2. **The certificate.** Each certificate carries an ID. Match it against
   [certificate-register.md](certificate-register.md). An ID that does not
   appear there was not issued by us.
3. **The presenter's own record.** Each presenter committed their own record to
   this repository under their own GitHub account. The commit history shows who
   added what, and when.
4. **The registration page.** Hosted by Eventbrite, dated independently of us.

Questions about any record here: learn@devdesignhq.com

We publish the real figures, including the ones that are not flattering.
Registrations and live attendance are reported separately because they are
different numbers.

## About Dev and Design HQ

Dev and Design HQ is a technology education company based in Abuja, Nigeria. It
runs cohort bootcamps and free programmes at the intersection of design,
software engineering, and AI-assisted product building.

The presenters in this series are students of the Ship and Found Bootcamp. They
were beginners when the cohort started. The recordings show the level they
reached.

- Website: https://devdesignhq.com

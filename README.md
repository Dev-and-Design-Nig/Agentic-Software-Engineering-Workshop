# Agentic Software Engineering Workshop Series

A free public online workshop series hosted by **Dev and Design HQ**

Students from the Ship and Found Bootcamp present live, hands-on technical
demonstrations to a public audience. Each presenter builds something in front of
the room and takes questions afterwards.

Agentic software engineering means building software by directing AI coding
agents and checking their work, instead of writing every line by hand.

Website: https://devdesignhq.com

This repository is the public record of the series. It holds the presenter
records, the materials used in each session, attendance figures, links to
recordings, and the certificate register.

## Verifying a presenter's claim

If you are checking a claim made by one of our presenters, for an application, an
admission, a role or a visa, everything you need is public.

1. **The recording.** Each edition's recording is linked in the table above. Each
   presenter's record links to the point in the recording where their own segment
   begins.
2. **The certificate.** Each certificate carries an ID. Match it against
   [certificate-register.md](certificate-register.md). An ID that does not appear
   there was not issued by us.
3. **The presenter's own record.** Each presenter has a record in
   [presenters/](presenters/) holding their session details and the prompts and
   documents they actually used on the night.
4. **The registration page.** Hosted by Eventbrite, dated independently of us.

Questions about any record here: learn@devdesignhq.com

We publish the real figures, including the ones that are not flattering.
Registrations and live attendance are reported separately because they are
different numbers.

## About Dev and Design HQ

Dev and Design HQ is a technology education company based in Abuja, Nigeria. It
runs cohort bootcamps and free programmes at the intersection of design, software
engineering, and AI-assisted product building.

The presenters in this series are students of the Ship and Found Bootcamp. They
were beginners when the cohort started. The recordings show the level they
reached.

- Website: https://devdesignhq.com
- Bootcamps: https://www.devdesignhq.com/bootcamps/product-design-engineering
